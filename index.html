Biography

I am Junhao Shen, a final-year undergraduate majoring in Data Science and Big Data Technology at the School of Mathematics and System Science, Xinjiang University. I am originally from Zibo, Shandong, China.

In fall 2027, I will join the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Xi’an Jiaotong University to pursue a master’s degree in Applied Statistics under the supervision of Prof. Zhandong Mei.

My research interests center on computer vision and deep learning, particularly video generation, world models, and 3D vision.

I am always happy to exchange research ideas and explore opportunities to collaborate.
